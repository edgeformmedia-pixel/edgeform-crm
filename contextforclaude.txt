HOW VIEWS ARE TRACKED (v4, replaces automated polling)
- No more automated fetching (YouTube API, TikTok/Instagram OAuth, Apify scraper) — all removed.
- Staff check each actively-tracking video's view count by hand, once a week, from the campaign's video
  list or the Affiliates page's "This week" checklist (GET /api/affiliate-videos?due=1), and enter it via
  the video detail modal (POST /api/affiliate-videos/:id/views).
- Earnings price on the DELTA since the last check, not the total: last week 10k, this week 20.2k -> the
  10.2k delta is what's priced and paid, at that week's CPM rate. Once a week is priced it's permanent —
  later rate/cap changes only affect future weeks.
- min_views_to_qualify is cumulative: weeks before a video's running total crosses it earn $0; the
  crossing week and every week after earn normally.
- A campaign going `paused` is a temporary hold (videos just drop off the weekly checklist, no earnings
  impact). Setting a campaign to `ended` permanently locks every still-approved video (final views frozen,
  no more entries taken) — this is now the ONLY thing that locks a video; there's no more per-video
  tracking window.
- Creators are paid weekly: any priced, unpaid week on an approved or locked video can go on a payout
  right away, not just once a video locks.
- Full design reasoning and the migration are in migrations/0021_affiliate_manual_views.sql and
  worker/affiliate-lib.js (priceEvents/recomputeCampaign).
